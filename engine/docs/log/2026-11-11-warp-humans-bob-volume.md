# The side-parted bob in three views

11 November 2026, Mancave, RTX 5070 Ti, Vulkan. The starting prototype on `4b935c60` fitted strands over the prior hair geometry. The scalp-rooted experiment still showed a corner behind the crown in profile and an opaque triangular patch at the forehead.

The new bob replaces its old hair cards and recessed crown with two fitted sections, one on each side of an off-centre part. Each uses 32 guide columns and two depth layers of eight control points. Ray intersections with the scalp triangles give the root position and the four face-shape deltas. A sheet inset in the guide volume carries the lower-density appearance. The shader interpolates the same volume for the outer fibres and computes their tangents along the curves. Projected player heads above 130 pixels gradually gain extra strands, up to 16,384 at 280 pixels. Spectators keep their existing count. The shared guide data for this style remains 24 KiB.

The visual fixture used slot two, match seed 1, a paused bot roster, arena 13 and noon lighting. Captures at 2560 by 1440 show the front, three quarters and profile using the same camera positions as the earlier experiment. A second fixture moved the camera through 41 angles at 1920 by 1080. The former notch at the crown is gone. The frontal part and the highlights still look more regular than in the SIGGRAPH research images. This experiment does not reproduce the authors’ renderer or establish a frame budget for multiple close players.
