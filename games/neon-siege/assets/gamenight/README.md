# Store media

`preview.mp4` (5 s, 150 frames, 960x540, 30 fps, silent H.264) and `preview.jpg` are real headless `warp script` captures of the Warp version with bots playing (software render in the cloud, so not GPU quality), cut into six shots. `gameplay.png` is the TV screenshot. The store keeps the cover and icon uploaded for the earlier LÖVE version. Guide: https://github.com/ontola/gamepad-party/blob/main/docs/gameplay-previews.md

2026-11-24: the clip and its poster are no longer kept in this repo (they are live in the store, uploaded through the API). To remake them, turn the capture above into a `preview.json` recipe next to gamenight.json and run `gamenight-publish.py clip --publish` (see the guide).
