# Car model

The street cars use Generic Toyama XR Sedan by mk2design, licensed under CC BY 4.0. This edition derives from the Sideways version. The chassis, four doors, bonnet, boot and wheels share one geometry buffer. Each removable panel follows the original mesh seams. Small generated inserts supply the backs of the panels, engine bay and boot well. Paint, glass and trim use materials adapted for Port Meridian, and the trim atlas is scaled to 256 pixels.
