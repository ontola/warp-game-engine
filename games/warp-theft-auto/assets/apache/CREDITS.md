# Apache

The fuselage, rotors, stores and original green livery are from *Attack Chopper* by Geibu3D. [Source and download](https://opengameart.org/content/attack-chopper) · [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Warp converted the original OBJ into three glTF parts, moved their pivots, reversed the forward axis and resized the green texture to 512 pixels. The two-seat arcade controls and playable turret are game additions.
